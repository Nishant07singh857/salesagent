# 5 Modern Sales Aggressors: Master Closing & Objection Handling Playbook

## 1. Alex Hormozi ($100M Offers/Sales)
**Core Closing Framework: C.L.O.S.E.R.**
- **C**larify why they are there (align goals).
- **L**abel their problem (establish authority).
- **O**verview their past pain (build urgency).
- **S**ell them the vacation (bridge current pain to desired future).
- **E**xplain away their concerns (address doubts).
- **R**einforce their decision (ensure commitment).

**Objection Handling Philosophy: Reframe, Don't Defend**
- **The 3 Layers:** Circumstances (time/money), Other People (spouses/bosses), Avoidance (internal fear).
- **"3 A's" Approach:** Acknowledge, Associate, Ask.
- **Proactive Coaching:** When prospects defer to partners/bosses, ask, "What is the one thing you think they will push back on?" to equip them for the internal conversation.
- **Rule:** Never justify pricing. Focus on widening the gap between their current pain and desired outcome.

## 2. Andy Elliott (Aggressive Car Sales/Closing)
**Core Closing Framework: High-Energy, Consequence-Driven**
- **Specific Closes:** 
  - *Change the Question Close:* Reframe customer focus.
  - *Now or Later Close:* Force urgency on timing vs. future commitment.
  - *Angel and Devil Close:* Address internal emotional conflict.
- **Eliminate Negotiation:** "Close on numbers" early by making the value so overwhelming that price becomes secondary.

**Objection Handling Philosophy: Mindset over Mechanics (80% Mindset)**
- **Pivot to Value/Appointment:** Immediately pivot away from early objections to reset the value proposition.
- **Consequence Questions:** Force the prospect to face the negative impact of *not* buying or solving the problem.
- **"I Need to Think About It":** Refuse to accept delays. Isolate the real cause (trust, money, authority) by questioning the logic of waiting.

## 3. Jeb Blount (Fanatical Prospecting)
**Core Closing Framework: Activity & The 30-Day Rule**
- **Mantra:** "Be Brief, Be Bright, Be Gone." Protect the buyer's time and respect the numbers game.
- **Emotional Detachment:** Treat objections as data points, not personal rejections. 

**Objection Handling Philosophy: The Turnaround Framework**
- Views early objections ("I'm busy", "Have a vendor") as knee-jerk reflexes, not real rejections.
- **3-Step Turnaround:**
  1. **Acknowledge:** Validate to lower defenses.
  2. **Disrupt (The "Ledge"):** Anchor the conversation away from conflict (e.g., "That's exactly why I called...").
  3. **Ask:** Pivot back to the call's purpose and ask for the next step.

## 4. Victor Antonio (Sales Velocity)
**Core Closing Framework: The Sales Velocity Formula**
- **Formula:** (Opportunities × Deal Value × Win Rate) / Sales Cycle Length.
- **Value-Centric Selling ("Value Trinity"):** Focus purely on increasing revenue, reducing costs, or expanding market share instead of price.

**Objection Handling Philosophy: "Blocking" Over Overcoming**
- **Preemptive Blocking:** "Overcoming" objections puts buyers on the defensive (Rule of Consistency). Instead, identify common objections and build counter-arguments directly into the pitch *before* the prospect can voice them.
- **Discount Defense:** Replace the behavioral reflex of discounting with structured value articulation to protect margins.

## 5. Ed Mylett (Persuasion/Max Out)
**Core Closing Framework: Question-Controlled Assumptive Closing**
- **Assumptive Posture:** Act as if the sale is guaranteed to happen, maintaining permission while guiding the close.
- **Alternative of Choice:** Give two options that both lead to a "yes" (e.g., "Would Tuesday or Thursday work better?").
- **Question Control:** Convert declarative statements into questions. The person asking questions controls the frame.

**Objection Handling Philosophy: Empathy & Redirection**
- **Acknowledge and Validate:** Never ignore an objection; it festers. Validate with "I know how you feel."
- **Feel, Felt, Found:** Connect their concern to a successful resolution (e.g., "Others felt that way, but here is what they found...").
