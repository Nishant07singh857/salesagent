# Customer Support Knowledge Base
(Apne business ke hisaab se edit karo — support agent yahi se jawab dhundta hai)

- Timing kya hai? Hamari timing subah 10 baje se shaam 7 baje tak hai, Monday se Saturday.
- Refund kaise milega? Refund 5-7 working days me original payment method pe aa jata hai.
- Appointment reschedule karna hai? Appointment wale number pe call karke reschedule ho jayega, 2 ghante pehle tak.
- Payment options kaunse hain? UPI, credit/debit card aur cash sab chalta hai.
- Location kahan hai? Address ke liye hamari website dekho ya WhatsApp pe location bhej dete hain.
